trial: Peapods LVF fWETH-12 / aspPEAS liquidation on Base.

This trial follows **confirmed** `Liquidate` logs on pair `0x23574154842bc86cf5bbc5f99376cfa1e2f82497`. After a take, it checks whether that borrower's remaining shares can still be liquidated profitably. It also rescans the last 10,000 blocks every 10 minutes and after a feed gap. It never reads the mempool. The route flash borrows WETH from Morpho, liquidates, redeems the seized aspPEAS, removes its LP through IndexUtils, sells pPEAS for fWETH in the public pool, redeems fWETH to WETH, repays Morpho, unwraps the surplus, and pays the owner. All steps are one transaction. `PeapodsTake.run` reverts unless WETH grows by `minProfit` and returns the gain to the bot as native ETH.

## Decision and cost

Each event carries the borrower, repaid shares, and WETH amount. The bot reads the source receipt to find the actual fWETH pool output; it scales that output to remaining borrow shares, tries conservative outputs, and simulates the entire executor call against head state. It sends only if the simulated native gain exceeds the signed gas cap. It uses at most a 3.2 million gas limit, 0.05 gwei max fee and 0.001 gwei priority fee. At a deliberately conservative ETH price of $4,000, the signed gas cap plus 100 billion wei for L1 data costs at most $0.641. The Base wallet is funded with $5, within the $18 OP Stack native cap. The priority fee budget is at most $0.0128 at $4,000/ETH; for the proven $3.14 gross gain it is under 0.5% of gross.

One 10-minute safety scan costs about 75 CU plus 10 CU for head, or 12,240 CU/day. The 78-event week implies about 11 events/day on average; per event budget one receipt (20 CU), two state calls (52 CU), fee read (20 CU), and up to 12 simulations (312 CU) = 4,444 CU/day average, with bursts bounded by the code's 100,000 CU/day cap. Feed maintenance is about 4 CU/min = 5,760 CU/day and about 35 CU per event = 385 CU/day average. Estimated total ~22,829 CU/day, $0.0121/day at $0.53 per million CU. The observed 68-event burst day would cost roughly 45,000 CU with the same assumptions. It waits on one low-volume `Liquidate` filter and a 10-minute safety timer; no per-block poll.

Machine-readable `logs/bot.jsonl` entries include detection, simulations, each send's won/lost status, gas used, and the winner's block. A signer refusal is logged and treated as final. The paper mode uses only an `eth_call` code override of the fork-deployed runtime; the live mode uses `EXECUTOR_ADDR_BASE` or `EXECUTOR_ADDR` from the gate.

Legal basis: the verified pair's public `liquidate(uint128,uint256,address)` explicitly transfers collateral to any liquidator that repays an insolvent borrower's debt. The route uses a public flash loan and ordinary redemption and pool swap, and only confirmed state. No pending transaction or price manipulation is used.
