# trial-kdiem-redeem-base

Cheap-chain trial (decision `2026-11-17-cheap-chain-trials.md`). Round trips through the two public doors of kDIEM
(wrapped Venice DIEM) on Base when an agent token's Uniswap v4 pools disagree with the DIEM price. No principal:
Morpho flash loan of USDC, profit in USDC.

## Mechanism

- **The doors.** Buffer `0x7eaef27fae47fee4f9bbe42e8e6421da8a334387` (proxy; implementation `0x763375ec…`, verified
  `src/venice/Buffer.sol`): `redeemDiem(kdiemIn, to)` burns kDIEM `0xf8b22f75b7ee248ff723650f43c98b253e7dfb60` and
  pays `kdiemIn` less the door fee (`doorFeeBps` = 100 = 1 pct, the maximum) in DIEM
  `0xf4d97f2da56e8c3098f3a8d538db630a2606a024` on the spot, bounded by the Buffer's DIEM (`balance()` = 13.17 DIEM,
  about 25,000 USD, at placement; `ExceedsBalance` otherwise). `mintKdiem(diemIn, to)` is the reverse door, same fee.
  Both are open to anyone; the source says the fee is there for the arbitrage bots that come through.
- **The pools.** kDIEM is the quote token of about 28 launchpad agent tokens (addresses ending `ca1`). Each has a
  hookless Uniswap v4 `T/kDIEM` pool and a hookless `USDC/T` pool at 1 or 2 pct (PoolManager
  `0x498581ff718922c3f8e6a244956af099b2652b2b`). DIEM trades against USDC on two V3-style pools,
  `0xf69cd40b…` (moving fee, 0.54-0.60 pct seen) and `0xbc323103…` (0.27 pct).
- **The take**, one transaction through the executor `DoorArb`:
  redeem: flash USDC -> T -> kDIEM (one v4 unlock, two hops), `redeemDiem`, DIEM -> USDC, repay;
  mint: flash USDC -> DIEM, `mintKdiem`, kDIEM -> T -> USDC, repay.
  `NoProfit` revert unless the USDC balance grew by `minProfit`; all USDC goes to the bot wallet.
  Fees on the two-hop route: 2 + 2 + 1 + 0.27 pct, so a gap needs about 5.3 pct between the two pools of a token.

## The bot

- Timer, one Multicall3 `eth_call` every 30 s: `doorFeeBps()`, `balance()`, `slot0()` / `token0()` / `fee()` of the
  two DIEM pools, and slot0 of 62 v4 pools through `PoolManager.extsload` (storage slot `keccak(id, 6)`, checked
  against a Swap event's `sqrtPriceX96`). `routes.json` (37 routes) is generated by
  `scratch/trial-kdiem-redeem-base/gen.py` from the PoolManager's `Initialize` logs.
- Screen in floats: marginal round trip after pool fees and the door fee above 0.3 pct. Many launch pools are
  one-sided and show a large edge that cannot be taken (`CurrencyNotSettled` 0x5212cba1); the bot simulates such a
  route once and remembers it by its pool prices, so a steady look is one call.
- A candidate is simulated with the exact `run()` (`eth_call`) at 20, 50, 100, 200, 400 USDC until the gain stops
  growing. Below 0.03 USDC: logged once as `below_interest`. Otherwise base fee, `eth_estimateGas`, gas limit =
  estimate x 1.2, priority fee = at most 1/10 of gross spread over the gas limit and at most 0.2 gwei, max fee = base
  fee x 1.25 + tip (never above 0.3 gwei), `cap = gasLimit x maxFee + L1 allowance`, in USDC at 2,500 USD/ETH.
  Sends only when gross > 1.3 x cap, with `minProfit = cap`.
- Log lines (`logs/bot.jsonl`): `start`, `heartbeat`, `check`, `opportunity`, `opportunity_skip`, `simulate_revert`,
  `paper_sim`, `signer_reject`, `send_result` (won / lost, gas used, send block, winner block, tx index), `read_budget`.
- Live: `EXECUTOR_ADDR_BASE` or `EXECUTOR_ADDR` required; the signer's address (`my_address()["address"]`) is `from`.

## Read cost

2,880 looks a day x 1 `eth_call` (26 CU) = 74,900 CU/day; a simulated route adds 26-130 CU, an opportunity about
90 CU more (block, estimate, guard call) plus receipts. About 78,000 CU/day = 0.041 USD/day at 1M CU = 0.53 USD.
Hard cap in code 95,000 CU/day, then sleep to 00:00 UTC. No feed filter (the pools' Swap event is the PoolManager's,
a busy emitter).

## Proof

`scratch/trial-kdiem-redeem-base/proof.py 60479755` (see `fork_evidence` in the manifest): Base fork at the parent of
operator B's take, no fixture for the success. NotOwner, NoProfit guard, success +0.458856 USDC to the owner (B took
0.167433 with the other DIEM pool), repeat reverts, losing direction reverts, mint path run mechanically.
`selftest.py`: paper path at head and at block 60479755 (same 458856 through the code override).

## Limits and risks

- The door is two days old and upgradeable (UUPS, owner = timelock per the source): fee, pause or interface can
  change; the bot stands down on a failed getter and every send is guarded by `minProfit`.
- 30 s timer against incumbents that often take in 5-30 s: the trial measures how many gaps outlive a look.
- Gains are small (0.1-0.5 USDC); the route list is fixed at placement (new agent tokens need a `gen.py` re-run).
