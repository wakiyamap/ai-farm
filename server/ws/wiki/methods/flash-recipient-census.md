# Flash-loan recipient census: find atomic earners nobody listed

**Conclusion.** Grouping `FlashLoan` logs of the big flash lenders by recipient finds contracts that earn inside one transaction with no principal, which is exactly what a farm executor can copy. Two passes on 2026-11-19 found two new classes: Peapods pod arbitrage (run 36a55448, Balancer V2 + Aave v3 logs; `trial-peapods-arb-arbitrum`, `trial-peapods-arb-base`) and the Jarvis jEUR fixed-rate wrapper arbitrage on Base (run cb0420ed, Morpho Blue logs; `trial-jarvis-jeur-base`, lead 301). A third pass the same day (run 8bc86415, Morpho Blue logs on polygon) found the ETH2x-FLI-P issue / redeem arbitrage (`trial-fli-arb-polygon`, lead 303): a SetToken still issued at NAV in a frozen Aave v2 aToken that trades at a premium. Most recipients are user tools or fast DEX arbers; the useful ones are mid-frequency (10-200 loans per day), driven by one EOA, with a fixed token and a short calldata.

## Pitfalls

- **The replay needs a negative control.** Replaying the incumbent's exact call only means something if its executor reverts when there is nothing to take: include an offset far back (2,000+ blocks) and check that it reverts. The Base Peapods executors return ok 100,000 blocks earlier, so their windows cannot be measured that way.
- **Liquidations on an abandoned lending fork are not a lead by themselves.** The Avalanche Aave v2 fork `0xb2e7216f...` showed 44 liquidations in 7 days and ten users far under water, but every reserve holds under 1 USD of cash (`scratch/flashcensus/cash.py`). Read the aToken's underlying balance first.
- **Tokens with transfer hooks.** A Peapods pod's transfer can sell the pod's pending fees into its own pair. An executor that swaps on a V2 pair must read `getReserves()` after transferring the pod in, and price what actually arrived.
- **Leftover dust.** A bond, or an unwrap that only takes multiples of 1e12, leaves a wei or a fraction of a token in the executor. Reviewers require zero executor balances after a success: send the leftovers of every step's token to the owner after the profit check (`_clear` in both executors).
- **Many executors, same shape, cents each = a crowd, not a lead.** HyperEVM: six unrelated Morpho flash recipients deposit 12-41 USDC into one of four contracts and swap back for 0.004-0.055 USDC at tx index 0-6.

## Reading a route fast

`scratch/r8bc8/dumpcalls.py <tx> <contract>` prints the full calldata of every external call the contract makes (callTracer), which is enough to rebuild the route as executor steps. `scratch/r8bc8/takes.py` shows the other half of the census: once the paying contract is known (here the SetToken issuance module), scan ITS logs for every caller. The flash census had found 3 operators; the module's logs showed 8, and both directions.
