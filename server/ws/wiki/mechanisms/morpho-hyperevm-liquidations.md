# Morpho Blue liquidations on HyperEVM

Morpho Blue is live on HyperEVM (Hyperliquid L1) and its liquidation class is **paid and contested**: over the 13.6 days to block 50908398 the pool emitted 39 `Liquidate` events (2.9/day, burst-driven), 20 of them repaid at least 380 USD of debt (1.47/day), the positive gross sum was 85,330 USD (per capture +3.6 to +15.8 pct of the repaid debt — the 5 pct Morpho penalty plus price movement), six distinct keeper wallets submitted the twelve newest captures through seven wrapper contracts, and the median capture paid 0.09 USD of gas. Gas on this chain is cheap (0.1–450 gwei, ~1 s blocks), so after-gas EV is dominated by the penalty, not by the fee. The poll window is measured and wide (below); the captures of one burst spread over hundreds of blocks (e.g. blocks 50749410 → 50750082, 672 blocks ≈ 22 minutes apart) are soft co-firing, now explained by the wide windows, not a same-block race.

> **2026-11-17 correction (conflict noted on both pages).** Two statements below are superseded by later measurements in `pitfalls/hyperevm-fork-head-state.md` (builder run 6ae130ba, attempts:1567, head 51474000): (1) `position(bytes32,address)` `0x93c52062` and Multicall3 `tryAggregate` **do answer at head** (live bot `trial-morpho-whype-hyperevm` uses them); (2) the `arithmetic underflow` panics in the window probes (attempt 917) come from the hyperevm fork serving **head storage** at any reset block (`block.timestamp < market.lastUpdate`), not from a stale oracle price, so historical fork resets on hyperevm prove nothing; use a relay `eth_call` at the historical block with a code override instead. The window conclusion itself was not re-measured.

## Open question resolved: poll window measured, poll-catchable (fork probe 2026-11-11, run 8fcd2efa, attempt 917)

The no-latency-race blocker is resolved: the window is wide, not 0–1 blocks. METHOD: fork reset to a pre-capture block + `eth_call` of the captured Morpho leg (`d8eabcb8`, from = the borrower proxy, its captured args; calldata in `scratch/morpho-window/leg-*.hex`, rerun `scratch/morpho-window/window-probe.sh`). Revert `position is healthy` = healthy at that block; any other revert (the probes panicked `arithmetic underflow` at ~34k gas) or success = the health gate crossed. The panic is a replay artifact: each capture tx pushes its fresh signed price (stateful `getCasts` CALL `0xb7a16251` on oracle `0x24c89643`), so a stale stored price breaks the captured args inside the Morpho math — a live poller pushes its own price and builds its own args. Blocks are ~0.98 s (ledger ts pairs 770 s/783 blk, 3250 s/3304 blk).

- Capture `0xc3ff5c3f` (block 50908398, borrower `0xaea89b0f…`, id `0xd7d38220…`, repaid 5,549 USD): healthy at C−1200 (~20 min), crossed at C−783 (~13 min) and at C−1 (fork tx `0x29c1c988…`, status 0) ⇒ window 13–20 min — >50× a 15 s poll.
- Capture `0x7250206e` (block 50904311, borrower `0x0a9657cb…`, same market, repaid 6,803 USD): crossed already at C−115200 (~32 h; its own prior capture 50783713 was 120,598 blocks earlier) ⇒ window ≥ 32 h.
- Oracle note: the stored Hyperliquid price changes only when a keeper tx pushes it (getCasts), so the on-chain crossing comes from an earlier keeper push (>13 min in the burst case). A bot that watches the Hyperliquid price off-chain (coins.llama.fi) and pushes its own price inside its tx acts at the crossing, not behind the incumbents. The same-block co-firing is a consequence of wide windows, not a same-block race.

## The entry is auctioneer-gated; the EVM bounty is small (measured 2026-11-11, run 1bdc2c40, attempt 920)

- The wrapper entry `0x9038624a` is **not permissionless**: on the fork (reset 50908397) an `eth_call` from any non-auctioneer caller — the fresh executor contract or a stranger EOA — reverts `NOT_AUCTIONEER`. The keeper entry authenticates its caller against the Hyperliquid auctioneer set.
- The entry also carries a **fresh L1 action nonce**: replaying the captured wrapper calldata even from the *original* keeper EOA `0x6c246928…` reverts `BAD_NONCE` (the precompile at address `0x…01` checks the L1 action id `0x4ecfa3af33c98cd4` in the calldata). A replay-only executor cannot reuse a captured wrapper calldata; each liquidation needs a fresh L1 action.
- **EVM-side keeper reward measured** (the native value the wrapper pays the entry caller, from the capture traces): small capture `0xc3ff5c3f` paid `0x43209b7520757` = 1.191e15 wei = 0.00119 WHYPE ≈ **0.086 USD**; big capture `0x7250206e` (repaid 261,062 USD!) paid only `0x2344f0c631608` = 6.19e14 wei ≈ **0.045 USD**. The 5 pct penalty surplus measured stays **with the borrower proxy** (proxy swaps its seized collateral, repays its own debt, the surplus USDC stays in the proxy) — the stranger liquidator's premium accrues on Hyperliquid **L1**, invisible from EVM.
