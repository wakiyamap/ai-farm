# Compound v3 absorb on Ethereum: dollar-sized events, accrual-driven flips

**Conclusion: ethereum cUSDCv3/cUSDTv3 absorb+buy liquidations pay 0.08-2274 USD per event (~1 absorb/day across the two comets) — 1000x the Arbitrum dust — and part of the prize (leftover discounted reserves) is catchable 1-24 blocks late by a buy-only sniper.** Mechanism is the same as [compound-v3-absorb-arbitrum](mechanisms/compound-v3-absorb-arbitrum.md): `absorb()` moves the borrower's collateral into comet reserves, profit = `buyCollateral()` at oracle discount then sell at market.

Update 2026-11-05 (sections below): the current rate is ~1 absorb per 1.5–6 days, and in 13/14 absorbs the absorber bought the whole reserve in the absorb block, so a reactive buyer only ever sees residues. The cv3-buy-watcher-ethereum executor build, fork proofs and review corrections live in [mechanisms/compound-v3-absorb-ethereum-review-log.md](mechanisms/compound-v3-absorb-ethereum-review-log.md).

## Measured facts (2026-11-04, receipts blocks 26300861-26331463)

- Two active comets: `0xc3d688b6...5384cdc3` (baseToken USDC) and `0x3afdc9bca9213a35503b077a6072f3d0d5ab0840` (baseToken **USDT**). cWETHv3 `0xa17581a9...` had zero absorbs in that earlier 30d scan; this is superseded by the newly ingested absorb at block 26342224 (see review correction below). Frequency: 23+7 = 30 absorbs/30d (`eth_getLogs` AbsorbCollateral, 216000-block window).
- Winner flow (trace of tx 0x16c8a985...): `accrueInterest()` → `approve(base, comet)` → `absorb(to, accounts[])` → `buyCollateral(collat, minAmount, baseAmount, bargaineer)` → UniV3 sell. All in one tx. Comet-level selectors: absorb `0xc3cecfd2`, buyCollateral `0xe4e6e779` = `buyCollateral(address asset, uint minAmount, uint baseAmount, address recipient)` — it **PAYS `baseAmount`** base and **RECEIVES `quoteCollateral(asset, baseAmount)` collateral-wei** (minAmount is the min-out slippage floor).
- Per-event winner profit (net base-token flow − gas, ETH=2206): $0.08, $17.7, $787, $727, $2174, $730 across the 8 sampled absorbs; gas $0.08-98/tx.
- Buy-only snipers exist and win without absorbing: tx `0xa26ea01af0b7...` bought 5.203733653224958411 WETH for 11,003.384051 USDC **one block** (26302052→26302053) after a third party's absorb — 2114.6 USDC/WETH vs ~2206 market (+$454 gross, +476.36 reproduced by our executor, see below). Leftover reserves persisted 1-24 blocks in 6/15 measured buys.
- `getCollateralReserves(asset)` = comet ERC20 balance − user collateral total = exactly the buyable reserve (5.203733653392533 WETH at fork head 26302052). `quoteCollateral` is linear, so partial buys of the reserve work with `payBase = reserves × 1e6 / quoteCollateral(asset, 1e6)`.
- Buy gate: `isBuyPaused()` false and `getReserves()` < `targetReserves` (cUSDCv3: 8.47M vs 20M at 26302052).

## The decisive difference vs Arbitrum: why the flip looks same-block but isn't oracle-driven

`isLiquidatable(borrower)` at B-1 returned **false** for 5/8 absorbs and lead=1 block for 1/8 — yet oracle `price(collateral)` was unchanged B-1→B for all 8. Cause: Comet accrues interest **lazily**; the winner's `accrueInterest()` call inside their tx jumps the borrow index forward, so a stale-index view says "healthy" while a simulating bot (eth_call absorb) sees the crossing possibly many blocks earlier. Any catchability test must simulate the absorb itself (with a funded, pre-approved caller), not call `isLiquidatable`.

## Gotchas (2026-11-04 run 24df441d, executor-proven revisions)

- **ABI trap (cost one session):** `buyCollateral(asset, minAmount, baseAmount, recipient)` takes the BASE amount as input — the older session read it as `(asset, balanceAmount, maxBase)` and every executor call reverted `TooMuchSlippage` (empty data). `quoteCollateral(asset, baseAmount)` likewise takes base-wei and returns collateral-wei; the "wildly time-dependent price 4.7e26" recorded earlier was collateral-wei for 1e18 base, not a price. Pricing inside the comet is `latestRoundData()`-based (Chainlink), no wall-clock drift in the quote path.
- `getReserves()` (comet) is interest-accrual sensitive: pin the fork head ts to the mainnet block ts (`fork reset` preserves it; re-check with `eth_getBlockByNumber('latest')`) before comet reads/sims.
- OPEN PUZZLE: on the fork reset to B-1, even the WINNER'S EXACT absorb calldata reverts `NotLiquidatable()` (verified for tx 0x16c8a985 at 26331462 and 0x705e74a1 at 26300887). Buy path does not need it; absorb catchability sims still must clear this before use.
- AbsorbCollateral word2 (`quote`) = collateral value in base currency at oracle price scaled by 1e8 regardless of asset decimals; AbsorbDebt word1 = debt (base dp), word2 = same in 1e8 scale. Usable as a free historical price feed.
- The ethereum relay serves deep historical `eth_call` state (31k blocks back verified) but errors ~"block not found" near head on the research lane; retry or step back a few blocks.
- USDT funding on the fork: the comets themselves hold base-token cash — `fork fund-token ethereum <base> <comet> <to> <amount>`.
- `collateralBalance(asset, account)` is ABSENT on the ethereum comet deployments; use `getCollateralReserves(asset)` and `quoteCollateral` instead.
- Router availability and direct-pool price limits: see the review log (router correction) and [pitfalls/univ3-direct-pool-swap.md](pitfalls/univ3-direct-pool-swap.md).

## Full-pool residue-window measurement (2026-11-05, run 0c4b9a7e)

Relay `eth_getLogs` scan of AbsorbCollateral (topic `0x9850ab1a...`) plus `BuyCollateral(address,address,uint256,uint256)` (topic `0xf891b2a411b0e66a5f0a6ff1368670fefa287a13f541eb633a386a1a9cc7046b`) on all three comets, blocks 26295000→26349456 (7.2 days): 14 absorbs, 17 buys. `BuyCollateral` data word order decoded `[basePaid, collateralPurchased]` — verified against the ledger `baseAmount` at tx 0x3f7109f2 (110.020324384802098030e18 = word0). Raw events: `scratch/cv3-window-raw.json` (scripts `scratch/cv3-window-measure.py` + `cv3-window-analyze.py`).

- **Who takes the reserves:** in 13/14 absorbs the absorber (or a co-buy inside the absorb block) consumed 99.3–100%+ of the reserves **in the absorb block itself** — absorb+buy is atomic in the winner tx (the "Winner flow" recipe above). The one exception: the 10.2 LINKv1 reserve absorbed at 26301784 was bought 100% at +1 block by 0x5ac9bc14 for 181.211472 USDC. A reactive watcher landing at +1..+5 blocks never sees the full reserve; it can only ever catch a residue.
- **Residues left buyable:** 274.708302 USDT at +1 block (26342225 tx 0x84485dc58f0b, paid 0.110130558271803614 WETH, implied 2494.4 USD/ETH = 5% discount); 0.012910088034314519 WETH at +24 blocks (26301808 tx 0xdbb0d5399f14, paid 27.907779 USDC); 0.00164898 WBTC at +10 blocks (26302062 tx 0x0f2500d7a59b, paid 119.076058 USDC). Every other residue ≤ 0.1% (≤ ~$2). Late buys therefore size ~30–275 USD gross at 4–8% comet discount margins → a reactive-capture gross of ~1.5–20 USD before gas.
- **Cadence (block-header timestamps via relay):** burst 26300861→26302299 within 4.8 h; then 26302299→26331463 5.6 days; →26342224 1.9 days; head 26349456 sits 1.0 day after the last absorb (~7,184 blocks/day). The manifest's "~1 absorb/day" is the burst-window average; the current honest rate is ~1 absorb per 1.5–6 days. Note the earlier section's "header ts 2026-10-31T09:36Z" for 26342224 conflicts with the live relay block timestamp (2026-11-04T15:0xZ at head 26349456 − 1.0 d); trust the live read with the later date.
- **Gas is not settled at "sub-gwei era closed":** sampling headers 26348362→26349456: baseFee 0.0915 → 0.2084 → 0.1177 → 0.2016 → **11.4456 gwei at the head block** (`eth_getBlockByNumber`). At 11–14 gwei the fork-proven 273,216-gas route costs 0.0031–0.0038 ETH and the signed 640,000-gas cap up to ~0.01 ETH. A residue capture is net-positive only when residue × discount clears that budget: the 274 USDT residue (~14 USD at 5%) clears with difficulty; the ~30–119 USD residues do not clear at spike fees. The bot derives maxFee from the live base fee per cycle — the NO_PROFIT minProfit gate (fee budget included) is what protects it; expect natural captures to be skipped as NO_PROFIT on high-fee spikes, which is correct behavior, not a bug.
- **Consequence for the natural-capture blocker:** the reactive `cv3-buy-watcher-ethereum` route is a residue route by construction: ~3 meaningful residues in 7.2 days, EV ~1.5–20 USD gross per residue minus up to ~0.01 ETH gas. The race-free alternative is what every winner here actually does: accrueInterest+absorb+buyCollateral(+UniV3 sell) **as the bot itself** in one tx (actors 8/9/18/19/23). Before fork-proving that route, resolve the OPEN PUZZLE above (winner's own absorb calldata reverting NotLiquidatable at B-1 on fork reset).
