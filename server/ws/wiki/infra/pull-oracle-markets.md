# Pull-oracle lending markets on farm chains (decision 2026-11-14-taker-types-pull-oracle item 2)

**Second edition 2026-11-15 (run c5bc11c2):** Dolomite (arbitrum, ethereum) and Fluid (ethereum, base, arbitrum) checked: 0 of 165 sampled captures carry a pull marker; neither is a pull market. Details in the section "Second edition" below.

**Conclusion (2026-11-14, researcher/opus run 095e4f2d, first pass):** on the markets the ledger covers, **no permissionless P (pull-oracle) taker was found**. The one P taker is permissioned: HyperEVM Morpho `0xf2314753…` (keeper EOA `0x6c246928…`) attaches a RedStone signed-price payload to every capture (25 of the 57 ledger HyperEVM Morpho liquidations, 2026-11-04..11-14, ~64% of the 11-08..11-14 nominal gross), but its entry reverts `NOT_AUCTIONEER` for any other caller (fork-proven, lead 145, `mechanisms/morpho-hyperevm-liquidations.md`). The big money on Base and Arbitrum is **O, not P**: in the 72 h crash window 97% of Base Morpho gross (144 txs, 2.36M USD nominal), 92% of Base Aave gross and 84% of Arbitrum Aave gross entered through **FastLane Atlas `metacall`** (`0x583dcfef…` base, `0x8ad1ae9d…` arbitrum), an auction for the right to liquidate. Euler v2, the main lender not in the ledger, showed no pull-oracle update in any of its 24 liquidations on Base and Ethereum over 7 days. The HyperEVM Aave-fork pools repeat the pattern: a second permissioned auctioneer wrapper (`0x7a0ea1f7…`, RedStone payload) takes half of their ~9.9k USD/7 d, and the only permissionless P taker found anywhere (`0x429b6057…`, Pyth update inside the tx) works a pool with ~14 USD gross in 7 d. Avalanche Euler and Ethereum Gearbox had 0 liquidations in the crash week. So no row reaches the "seconds > R and >= 300 USD/30 d" head of the list; open rows are listed at the end.

Pull markers checked in each capture tx (all three needed, any one = P): Pyth `PriceFeedUpdate(bytes32,uint64,int64,uint64)` log `0xd06a6b7f…` in the receipt; Chainlink Data Streams `ReportVerified(bytes32,address)` log `0x58ca9502…`; the RedStone payload marker `000002ed57011e0000` in the tx calldata. Control: the same Pyth topic returns 412 logs over ~1 day of Base (blocks 0x363055d-0x369992d; Pyth contract `0x8250f4af4b972684f7b336503e2d6dfedeb1487a` 151, others `0x3f93e342…` 221, `0xbc16aee6…` 40), so a zero is a real zero. B candidate = a separate tx emitting Chainlink `AnswerUpdated` / Pyth / Data Streams at most 3 positions before the capture in the same block. Scripts: `scratch/pull-r095e/` (`types.py`, `routes.py`, `ptype.py`, `hyper.py`, `trace.py`).
