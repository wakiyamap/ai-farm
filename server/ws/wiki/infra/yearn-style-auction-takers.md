# Yearn-periphery-style Dutch `Auction` contracts (AuctionKicked): alive, professionally contested (2026-11-11)

**Conclusion.** Contracts with the Yearn tokenized-strategy-periphery `Auction` ABI sell strategy reward/fee tokens for a `want()` token through a Dutch price, and anyone may `take` with a callback. On Ethereum the class is busy: **369 `AuctionKicked(address,uint256)` events in 30 days over 42 auction contracts, and 330 take-topic txs**. It is already taken by at least 8 specialist searchers at thin margins. **Not worth a farm bot. A limited Ethereum observation source was installed in follow-up run 3f1858 on 2026-11-11.** Measured as a quick census only (run 1a4364c4, about 15 min). Lot values per day were not summed.

## Why not
Margins are about 0.01% of the lot, and an incumbent with 52% share plus at least 7 other searchers already crosses each auction at break-even with aggregator routing. That is a latency/routing race (decisions/human/2026-11-06-no-latency-race.md). Reopen only on evidence of economically significant auctions with delayed or missing takers. The follow-up below tested the original cheaper-chain next step.

Official [Auction.sol](https://github.com/yearn/tokenized-strategy-periphery/blob/master/src/Auctions/Auction.sol) resolves `0x3539af76...` as **AuctionSettled(address indexed from)**. `_take` emits it when the full available lot is taken; a separate unpaid `settle(from)` can emit the same event after the token balance becomes zero. It is **not a record of every partial fill**, and tx_from is not automatically a profitable taker. Callback support sends tokens before pulling payment, permitting an atomic sale/payment route; no bot or executor was built.

Installed `yearn_periphery.auction_settled` on the 20 busiest Ethereum emitters selected from the prior researcher's 30-day kick snapshot: **322/369 kicks (87.3%)**, not 87.3% of profit or settlements. All 20 have nonempty 45-byte clone runtime at the current relay head. A follow-up historical layout check found **291 completion logs across all 20 selected emitters**, each with exactly two topics and empty data, confirming indexed `from_token`. Source has no numeric amounts and uses tx_from discovery attribution. It observes auction completion, not P&L. Check initialization and cursor/head coverage before using any zero. At 19:17 UTC initialization was still pending (0/20), before a verified ingestion tick for this new source; this is a handoff check, not a zero-activity result. At steady head, one Ethereum address-array log request costs nominally 60 CU/tick = **5760 CU/day** at 15-minute ticks, plus tx_from lookups (roughly 20 CU per distinct uncached transaction); initialization/backfill uses the existing agent-source budget. No budget increase, account or fee is needed.
