# Compound v2 mainnet is alive at new token addresses; Aave v2 dust liquidation pays a dead token

A zero-code probe on a handful of historical mainnet addresses is not a dead_end for a whole class. As of 2026-11-05 the old-pool pitfall recorded here read `eth_getCode = 0x` at the Compound v2 Comptroller `0x3d9819210A31B4Ed2c0295730E7790Ff3b21Ea8b`, the legacy Comptroller `0x398eC28dc777dC6d95B9e83F91C0015102394A7f`, cUSDC `0x39AA39c02219824a192381d2AE6335C07447A495` and cETH `0x4ddc2d193aaf8ce8c7dea8009088883759c84767`, and concluded the class was gone. That probe hit withdrawn/deprecated token contracts, not the live markets.

Measured 2026-11-06 (run 8cb73149, relay research lane): the live mainnet cToken markets carry code and liquidate every day. Gross per liquidation is repay x penalty <= ~0.13 USD, ~2 USD/day for the whole class before gas: the compound_v2 mainnet whale-hunt is a **dead_end**, and no liquidatability-window probe is needed.

The new mainnet deployment also breaks the legacy getter ABI: at the parent block `borrowBalanceStored` (`0x67c98977`), `exchangeRateStored` and `exchangeRate` REVERT, and `0xaa5af0fd` returns the market borrowIndex (mantissa ~1.475e18), not `borrowBalanceCurrent`. Dust gross is 0.0246 USD/liquidation (10 pct of the median 0.246 USD debtToCover), below the measured 359k gas/liquidation = 0.08-0.12 USD at the current 0.09-0.14 gwei floor; the cohort cadence collapsed to 4 events in the last 20000 blocks with zero stETH-collateral captures (the one-shot cohort is swept); and the whale tail is a measured 0-1 block same-block race (liquidatable at N-1, healthy at N-1201) that the farm does not chase.

**Measuring whether a token has a venue before building a sale leg:** `eth_getLogs` the token's own `Transfer` topic over the last ~40000 blocks. Zero transfers = zero venue activity anywhere, whichever routers and pools you remember; the token is inventory, not cash. Nonzero transfers at least prove somebody trades it — then check the specific pool.

Rule: before recording a protocol class as dead on a chain, enumerate emitters from events (topic-only scan of a known event hash), then eth_getCode the emitters. An address you remember is the address someone deprecated. And before counting an event field as dollars, diff one transaction: raw event words are shares and indices as often as they are USD.
