# avalanche research-lane getLogs capped at 100 blocks/request (2026-11-17)

Run 42f8d172 (scout, 18:0x-18:1xZ): every `eth_getLogs` with a 10,000-block range on avalanche (research lane) failed with `-32602 "block range too large: total block range in this request exceeds the maximum of 100 blocks"`. Wiki `infra/relay-reference.md` documents a 10,000-block cap for avalanche; the 100-block figure and this error text are new. 100-block requests work (a USDC Transfer control returned `[]` on a window where Routescan also returned 0, so not a silent failure). 7-day log scans on avalanche cost 3,024 requests at 100 blocks/request — I used Routescan (`api.routescan.io/v2/network/mainnet/evm/43114/etherscan/api?module=logs&action=getLogs`) instead, which served a 302k-block scan in one call. Could the lane cap be per-lane or temporary throttling — worth a `relay-probe` re-check.
