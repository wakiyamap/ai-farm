# Aave V4 hub/spoke liquidations

**Conclusion.** Aave V4 moves the liquidation battleground off the pool you can read off a DefiLlama adapter: the adapter lists only the **Hub** (on Base, the Equities Hub 0xa4d5947Eb727A052bae69C593FfC84247EC9864E), while liquidations execute on the **Spoke** - and the hub exposes no enumerator for its spokes, so the address hunt must come from the hub's own accounting logs (its `Add/Draw` events index the spoke as an address topic). Measured 2026-11-06 on Base: the spoke is 0x17905db0e4a3514467539956c084180616ae7b8d. **The Base liquidation pond is a same-block-race pond: none of its five 14-day captures was poll-catchable** (measured 2026-11-07, below), so it belongs to the fast infrastructure we do not run (`decisions/human/2026-11-06-no-latency-race.md`).

**Liquidation mechanism** (from the aave/aave-v4 repo, docs/overview.md + src/spoke/Spoke.sol). Anyone can call `liquidationCall(collateralReserveId, debtReserveId, user, debtToCover, receiveShares)` except the borrower themselves (no keeper role gate). Instead of V3's close factor, the liquidator repays only up to the **Target Health Factor** and seizes collateral at a **Dutch-auction bonus that grows as the borrower's health factor drops**: between `liquidationBonusFactor x maxLB` at the liquidation threshold and `maxLB` at `healthFactorForMaxBonus`, with a protocol fee taken from the bonus. `HEALTH_FACTOR_LIQUIDATION_THRESHOLD` is exactly `1e18`, so liquidatability = `healthFactor < 1`. A hard `DUST_LIQUIDATION_THRESHOLD` of 1000 USD in value units forces full-repayment paths for small positions. The event `LiquidationCall(uint256,uint256,address,address,bool,uint256,uint256,(int256,int256,uint256),uint256,uint256,uint256)` (topic0 0x2a1f12d996f5...) is emitted on the spoke and carries `debtAmountRestored` in debt-asset units - a ready-made cadence + per-event-size source. `getLiquidationBonus(reserveId, user, healthFactor)` reverts (`arithmetic underflow`, panic 0x11) for `healthFactor >= 1e18`: the getter only values a position already below the threshold.

**Measured flow on Base (Equities Hub).** 14-day scan to head 54092583: 5 liquidations (0.36/day), all in one burst of 2.4 days (blocks 53550168-53652038), then 10 days of silence; debt repaid per event 3.11-150.07 USDC, total 383.35 USD (debt reserve 7 = USDC 6-dec); all five won by one watcher, the farm-known multi-protocol liquidator 0x5ac9bc14376bf2727c6211960cf3cec1e2f295bd. Winner-gas per tx 546k-840k gasUsed (cheap at the 0.005 gwei basefee of the day, ~0.02 USD). Liquidator share of seized collateral shares = 99.53 percent of the removed amount in all five events -> the `liquidationFee` term is exactly 1000 bps: `1 - fee x (bonus-10000)/(bonus x 10000)` fits 0.9953 for fee 1000 bps (10 percent of the bonus goes to the protocol), not 100 bps.

**Window and bonus params measured (2026-11-07, run 139d7e13, lead 42 closure, attempts 347).** Fork HF ladder (reset each capture block minus E-k, `getUserAccountData(user).healthFactor`, selector 0xbf92857c, k up to 128): the five users crossed `HF < 1` only at the edge of their capture block - HF 1.0039 / 1.0032 / 1.0011 (healthy) at E-1 and even at E-128 for the three big events, liquidatable only at E-2/E-1 (0.9950) for one and only at E-1 (0.9994) for the other. Poll-catchable captures = 0/5, windows 0,0,0,2,1 blocks - the same oracle-update crossing that made every aave-v3 watcher `blocked_infra` (`pitfalls/aave-threshold-crossing-arbitrum.md`). Config read on the spoke: `getLiquidationConfig` = targetHealthFactor 1.24, healthFactorForMaxBonus 0.9, liquidationBonusFactor 9000; per-reserve `maxLiquidationBonus` derived as 10550 bps from the two `getLiquidationBonus` returns (10497 at HF 0.994952 and 10495 at 0.999400 fit the `calculateLiquidationBonus` interpolation with maxLB 10550 exactly) -> realized bonus ~5 percent minus the 10 percent protocol fee on it = ~4.5 percent net, whole-pond gross ceiling 383.34 x 0.045 = ~17 USD / 14 d = **1.2 USD/day**. Even with fast infrastructure the pond pays less than one watcher session; keep it behind the fast-infra restart condition.
