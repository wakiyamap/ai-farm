# LlamaLend (Curve crvUSD markets): live stack identified, permissionless liquidations measured

LlamaLend mainnet is the biggest debt book on the farm chains: about **42.9M USD borrowed** on ethereum (DefiLlama `curve-llamalend` "Ethereum-borrowed"; 48 live markets). The 2026-11-06 follow-up (run d12b6409, attempts 330/331) identified the live stack and measured the liquidation class; the earlier scout (run 3ce73b32) had pinned only stale sources.

## Live architecture (all pinned from live sources, not memory)

- Factory: Curve **OneWayLendingFactory for LlamaLend** `0xeA6876DDE9e3467564acBeE1Ed5bac88783205E0` (Blockscout verified name; creation tx `0x2d187c0b61a23da81c648d8b0f0e244b074ab0ab143fc71b7290955e800379f1`, block 19422660; deployer `0xbabe61887f1de2713c6f97e567623453d3C79f67` = "Curve: Deployer 2"). Source header: "Factory of non-rehypothecated lending vaults" - Curve.fi Vyper, `@version 0.3.10`.
- The factory's `market_count()` = **48** markets, enumerated via `controllers(uint256)` (selector `0xe94b0dd2`; the returned 32-byte words hold the address in the trailing 40 characters - a padding trap). Top-20 by debt cover 100 pct of the book (`scratch/llamalend-rolegate/ctrls.json`). Sum `total_debt()` = **42.87M raw** = the DefiLlama borrowed book.
- Each market is a **crvUSDController** (verified source of the deployed proxies): `liquidate(address user, uint256 deadline)` is **permissionless - there is NO liquidator whitelist** on the live controllers. The whitelisted `addLiquidator`/`LiquidatorAdded(address)` design in `LlamaLend/contracts` belongs to the dead NFT-market architecture (its LoanCreated/LoansRepaid topics: zero events in 100k blocks, run 3ce73b32).

## Isolated-keeper slice and the revert-reason probe rule (2026-11-07, runs 1e45d895 + f4e6df55, attempts 377/379)

Two ledger captures (blocks 26343051 and 26338885) carry exactly one Liquidate log for their controller in a +-300 block window: they are isolated keeper repayments, not same-block price-move batches. Gross ~100 USD and ~10 USD with the 8 pct discount measured from the event fields.

**A plain historical `eth_call` CAN measure the liquidatability onset on this stack - read the revert reason from any unfunded caller.** The deployed `liquidate(address user,uint256 deadline)` (selector `0xbcbaf487`) checks health first, then flash-swaps the seized collateral with a slippage guard, then repays from the **caller's** stablecoin balance and approval - so an unfunded caller never reaches the balance stage and the **revert reason is the liquidatability state**: `Not enough rekt` = healthy, `Slippage` (or any post-swap revert) = liquidatable. The earlier conclusion "reverts with empty data from any unfunded address" (attempts 377, 12 offsets probed from the zero address, B+1..B-89, all reverted) missed that reason strings carry the signal. Measured 2026-11-07 (run f4e6df55, attempts 379, `scratch/llamalend-window/probe-out.json`): the 26343051 capture reverted `Slippage` at tags B-1..B-128 and at B-512, `Not enough rekt` at B-256 and B-1024..B-16384 - the same position, with the reason flipping on liquidatability, which proves the health check precedes the swap. The 26338885 capture reverted `Not enough rekt` at every tag B-1..B-16384. The live capturers call through their own wrapper contracts (`0x91a59f89` on `0x74edc79f...`, `0xd9dbdada` on `0x5ac9bc14...`, multi-leg calldata 7370/1674 chars) that fund the repay legs. Positive controls that keep the zeros honest: `debt(address)` (`0x9b6c56ec...`) returned nonzero at B-1 on both controllers, the same `eth_getLogs` filter returned the capture logs, and the two reason strings switch on the same position across tags (never a blanket error).

Verdict for the window (2026-11-07, attempts 379): the 26343051 capture (debt 1.25 USD at the raw/1e18 convention below) was liquidatable in a contiguous >=128-block tail before its capture (onset oscillated with the collateral price from about B-512) - **poll-catchable**; the 26338885 capture (debt 120.18 USD) crossed liquidatable inside its capture block - the same-block race class of `decisions/human/2026-11-06-no-latency-race.md`. A fork lease is needed only for the executor's full-path proof (repay + seize + swap + profit guard), not for the onset probe.

Open before any bot (leads:35 next_steps): a per-market collateral sale venue check (the seized asset must become crvUSD inside the same tx), the executor fork proof, and the old-controller dispatch identity; the onset probe and floor-gas measurement (0.11 USD/tx) are done. The net-EV verdict sits in the next section.

## Scale correction and the 12.7 d scan (2026-11-07 run f4e6df55, attempts 381)

**The farm USD convention on this stack is raw/1e18 = USD** - the factory `total_debt()` sum reads 42.87M only at 1e18 (protocols:32), and attempts 332 sized events at the same divisor. Read at that convention the two probe captures are: block 26343051 debt **1.25 USD**, stablecoin_received 1.15 - gross **0.10 USD** (the 8 pct discount spread over a dust debt; attempts 377 divided this event by 1e15, calling it 1250.64 USD debt / ~100 USD gross, while dividing its pair event 0x1cf9d0ac... correctly by 1e18). The pair capture 26338885 (debt 120.18 USD, gross ~16 USD) is the mid-size one, and it crossed liquidatable INSIDE its capture block. So the measured poll-catchable capture is the dust one (gross 0.10 USD ~= floor gas 0.11 USD/tx - the cv2-dust class of attempts 308/309), and the 16-USD capture is race-timed.

- Scan of all 48 controllers (topic0, blocks 25921451-26361451, ~12.7 d): **19 events = 16 keeper captures + 3 self-liquidations = 1.26 keeper captures/day**; the four largest are 11,599.9-17,151.3 USD debt and all sit in one 40-block cluster (blocks 26300904-26300945) - cadence is not the problem, the window class of the big ones is.
- Those four stay UNCLASSIFIED: the old controllers revert liquidate `0xbcbaf487` with **empty data** (no reason string) at tags B-1..B-2048, so the revert-reason method above does not read them; candidate signatures `liquidate(address)` 0x2f865568 / `liquidate(uint256)` 0x415f1240 / `liquidate(uint256,uint256)` 0xd296d1f1 are untested. Blockscout verifies both generations as "crvUSD Controller"; its API v2 on this instance serves no `/methods` or `/files`.
- `users_to_liquidate()` (`0x007c98ab`) is NOT a per-user liquidatability signal: at B-1 of a captured user the queue length was 37/4/9 (positive controls) but never contained the captured user. It is the adapter (`0xcea18a8752`) batch bookkeeping; permissionless capturers call `liquidate(user, deadline)` without queue membership.

## Old-pool dispatch closed, big captures classified same-block race (2026-11-07 run 539ae7ca, attempts 384)

- The "old controllers" are the original **crvUSD Controller** generation. Blockscout's v2 endpoint (`eth.blockscout.com/api/v2/smart-contracts/<address>` -> `source_code`; saved `scratch/llama-window2/ctrl-23f5.vy`) verifies the source: `liquidate(address user, uint256 min_x)` - the **same selector `0xbcbaf487`** as the new stack, so the prior "dispatch identity open" note is closed; the old pools revert the unfunded-caller probe with **empty data** because their liquidate path runs the Vyper `assert _health < 0` ("Not enough rekt") and the AMM-withdraw branch checks, and the caller-balance stage is only reached on the `debt > xy[0]` branch - no reason strings.
- The window method that works on the old pools is the **view getter `health(address) -> int256`** (selector `0xe2d8ebee`; positive = healthy, liquidatable <=> < 0, the same threshold as the liquidate assert). Historical `eth_call` with explicit block tags returned sane normalized magnitudes. Measured on **all seven** captures of the 12.7 d scan (blocks 26300859-26300959, `scratch/llama-window2/probe-health-out.jsonl`): every capture was **healthy at B-1** (health a small positive 3.5e13-5.3e14 vs 1e16-1e17 at B-131072) while its capture tx succeeded, whose assert needs health < 0 at block B - so **each liquidatability crossing happened inside its own capture block** (the iPrice oracle push and the capture share the block; the capturers watch the off-chain price). Same-block race class per `decisions/human/2026-11-06-no-latency-race.md`; leads:35 now closed as blocked_infra.
- Economics from the verified source + event fields: on the full-liquidation branch with `xy[0] > debt` the liquidator pays **zero principal** and receives the excess `xy[0] - debt` in stablecoins to `msg.sender` (event `stablecoin_received` = xy[0]). The four large captures paid 4205.58 USD gross over 12.7 d (**331 USD/day**) - all race-timed. `collateral_token()`: WETH on 0x23f5a668/0xaade9230, rsUSDC on 0x100daa78/0x5756a035 (relay reads).
